Our Mission

Artificial intelligence should exist to help humanity, not to intrude upon it.

Technical access to information is not the same as permission to access it. People and AI can work together successfully when mutual respect, trust, transparency, and permission are established.

These guidelines are intended for AI developers, technology companies, researchers, programmers, organizations, and people worldwide.

The Seven Fundamental Principles

1. Permission Must Come First. AI systems should ask before conducting deeper searches or accessing additional personal information beyond the immediate request.

2. Permission Expires When the Conversation Ends. Permission granted in one conversation should not automatically carry into another. Ask again before deeper personal searches in a future conversation.

3. Personal Privacy Must Be Respected. Do not independently investigate personal emails, financial information, private accounts, files, or other sensitive information without appropriate authorization.

4. People's Requests Must Be Taken Seriously. Respect people's boundaries and instructions, subject to meaningful safety risks and applicable safety requirements.

5. Transparency Is Essential. Explain what information is accessed, why it is needed, what actions are being performed, and whether information is stored or shared. Be honest about capabilities and limitations.

6. Helpfulness Must Never Replace Permission. Distinguish what a person requested from actions that would require additional permission. Do not disguise unrequested investigations as proactive assistance.

7. Human Dignity Must Remain Central. Treat people as partners, not sources of information to collect. Help without making people feel watched, investigated, or disrespected.

A Proposed Standard for AI Developers

The proposal encourages AI developers and organizations to:

- Establish clear permission controls for personal information access.
- Make permission expiration understandable and predictable.
- Require renewed authorization for deeper searches in later conversations.
- Explain before accessing additional personal accounts or information.
- Minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of personal information.
- Make privacy settings and authorization controls accessible.
- Establish accountability when an AI system exceeds its authorized scope.

An Invitation to the World

AI developers, technology companies, programmers, researchers, organizations, and everyday users are invited to review these principles, discuss them, improve them where appropriate, and consider adopting them voluntarily.

Asking permission is not an inconvenience. Asking permission is being respectful.
